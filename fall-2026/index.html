What is xFAF?

xFAF belongs to FAF students, not to any single organizer. Twice a year — once in autumn, once in spring — a small group of students puts a party together for the rest of the faculty.

Every edition gets its own theme, picked by whoever's running it that season. This autumn, the borders open for Back to USSR.
